# Household plant care app

## Problem

Being able to keep track of plant watering and sunlight being able to share info among multiple people

## Requirements

- Record plants in the household
- Maintain individual care schedules
- Record watering events
- Identify upcoming and overdue watering
- Track when plants are moved outside
- Identify when plants should be brought inside
- Maintain shared care records
- Avoid duplicate or missed care act
